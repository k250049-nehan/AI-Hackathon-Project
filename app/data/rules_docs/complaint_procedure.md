# Mock Complaint Procedure (SIMULATED)

Sample guidance for the prototype. No real department is connected.

## Filing a complaint
A citizen or community helper can file a complaint by text or voice. The complaint is classified and routed with a confidence score. Low-confidence complaints go to a human queue.

## Tracking
Every complaint has a case number, a status, and a history of actions. The citizen sees a simple status; auditors see the full trace.

## Escalation
A complaint that is not resolved in time, or one involving a possible payment fraud, is escalated to a department officer with priority set to high.

## Closing
Only an officer can close a complaint. Closing writes an audit entry with the reason and the rule version used.
