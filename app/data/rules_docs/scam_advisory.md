# Mock Scam Advisory (SIMULATED)

Sample guidance for a prototype. Message classification is local and heuristic.

## Relief payment scams
Fraudulent relief messages often promise a payment and ask the citizen to click a link, share an OTP, or send a CNIC photo. A message that only mentions a relief payment is not automatically a scam; at least two independent signals are required before blocking.

## What to do
Do not click unknown links. Do not share an OTP, PIN, password or CNIC photo in reply to a message. Forward suspicious messages to the platform's scam-report flow so a reviewer can check them.

## False alarms
Blocking a real citizen is a real cost. When scam evidence is weak, the system asks for more information or hands the message to a human instead of blocking it. False alarms are counted on the evaluation dashboard.

## Reporting
A reported message is classified, stored with its confidence score and area, and shown on the mock scam map by type, district and time.
