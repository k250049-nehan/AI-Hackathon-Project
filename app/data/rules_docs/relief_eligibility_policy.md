# Mock Relief Payment Eligibility Policy (SIMULATED)

This document is bundled sample data for a prototype. It is not a real government policy.

## Who can apply
An applicant may be a widow, a person with a disability, an unemployed person, or a low-income worker. The applicant must be a resident of the district stated on the claim.

## Income limits
A household with monthly income of 30,000 rupees or less may qualify. Households above this limit are referred to a department officer for manual review. Income is declared by the applicant and cross-checked against the mock data service.

## Required documents
Every claim needs a valid CNIC. A proof of income or income certificate is requested when the applicant's income is above 20,000 rupees or when the declared documents are missing. Blurry documents are sent for manual checking instead of being rejected automatically.

## Claim amount
The maximum automated claim amount is 25,000 rupees. A larger claim is escalated to a human officer. The amount must be reasonable compared with declared income.

## How decisions are made
A claim is auto-approved only when identity is verified, the rule checks pass, and confidence is at least 0.90. Confidence between 0.65 and 0.90 asks for more information. Confidence below 0.65 is handed to a human reviewer. Every decision records the rule version used.

## Duplicate claims
One claim per CNIC is allowed in a 30-day window. A repeated claim is flagged for review rather than cancelled automatically.

## Payment step
No payment is released by the AI. Mock payment disbursement always requires an officer's approval after the decision is reviewed.
